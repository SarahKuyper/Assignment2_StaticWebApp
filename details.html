Bellamie

Abilities: None

Backstory: She's inspired by peacock spiders but I wouldn't be supprised if she colored her fur to be more neon. She lives with her boyfriend, Lucas, and works as an independent mechanic. She is a humanoid arachnae (taken after her human fater) which is a bit unusual for "normal" arachnae (human upper body with a spider lower body). To help with her work, she created a mechanical spider body. This mech suit allows her to use her silk in her work; holding lights, body parts, or the entire project. With it as well she is able to move and work around the project in a 3D space. My favorite part about her are her black teeth (because spider fangs are black).

Interests: Raves/parties, her work, anything STEM
